---

## 💻 SQL Commands Practiced

- `ROUND()` – Rounds numbers to specified decimal places.
- `CEIL()` – Rounds numbers upwards.
- `CEILING()` – Alternative to CEIL().
- `FLOOR()` – Rounds numbers downwards.
- `ABS()` – Returns the absolute value.
- `MOD()` – Returns the remainder after division.
- `POWER()` – Calculates the power of a number.
- `SQRT()` – Calculates the square root.
- `TRUNCATE()` – Removes decimal places without rounding.
- `SIGN()` – Returns the sign of a number.
- `RAND()` – Generates random numbers.
